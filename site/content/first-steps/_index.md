Start here. Every school begins with the **Grimoire** — craft it first. It documents every block, item, and mechanic as you unlock them, written in the voice of an unnamed scholar.

## Verdant Path

1. Find **Mystical Flowers** in the world and break them for **Petals**
2. Fill the **Apothecary** with water, throw in petals and a seed — craft a **Pure Daisy**
3. Place the Pure Daisy near stone to grow **Living Rock**, near logs for **Livingwood**
4. Craft a **Mana Pool** and place generating flowers nearby — mana starts flowing

---

## Summoning Path

1. Craft a **Scythe** — your primary tool for harvesting soul essence from fallen enemies
2. Fill the **Apothecary** with lava and begin learning Summoning recipes
3. Use harvested essence to fuel contracts and bind what you summon

---

## Sanguine Path

1. Craft an **Anathema** — the self-harm instrument that powers Sanguine magic
2. Fill the **Apothecary** with blood and begin the transformation recipes
3. Sacrifice fuels everything. Cost is the mechanic.

---

## Rational Path

1. Build a basic **Create** rotational setup — kinetic power is the foundation
2. Craft the **Workshop Table** and begin processing raw materials into precision components
3. Build a **Grounding Pylon** to generate Rational field and suppress local magical interference

---

## Rituals

1. Get **Lard** from a pig using a **Butcher's Knife**
2. Combine with Bone Meal to make **Chalk Lumps** — compress into ingots or blocks
3. Drop chalk into a mana pool to imbue it, then draw your first pattern on polished stone
