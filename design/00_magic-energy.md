# MAM — Magic Energy System

Cross-school energy infrastructure. Defines the two energy types, how they interact, the tier branching at T3, and how shared stations (Apothecary, Altar) serve all magic schools.

---

## Two Energy Types

| Energy | Colour | School | Pool |
|--------|--------|--------|------|
| **Mana** | Green | Verdant (primary producer) | Mana Pool |
| **Nox** | Purple | Cross-school dark energy (any non-Verdant school) | Desecrated Pool |

Both energies follow the same pool mechanic — generated by school-specific structures (flowers, dark sources TBD), stored in pools, consumed by infusion recipes and ritual stations.

---

## Energy Conversion — Tainting

Mana and Nox are mutually hostile. The moment any Nox enters a pool, all mana converts to Nox instantly at 1:1:

```
800,000 mana + any Nox  →  800,000 Nox  (instant, complete)
```

The quantity of energy is preserved — nothing is lost. But it is now Nox and cannot be used as mana. No partial taint state exists.

This creates:
- **Risk** — any Nox near a Verdant player's pool converts their entire mana supply instantly
- **Intent** — deliberately tainting a mana pool converts the quantity to Nox, bootstrapping Desecrated-tier production for any school
- **Cross-school tension** — dark players working near a Verdant sanctuary can corrupt it irreversibly

---

## Tier Progression

The material tier system branches at T3. Infused is the shared gateway; Sacred and Desecrated are parallel end-tiers.

```
Living Rock / Livingwood
  └─ Infused Living Rock / Infused Livingwood
        ├─ Sacred Living Rock / Sacred Livingwood      (Mana — Verdant branch)
        └─ Desecrated Living Rock / Desecrated Livingwood  (Nox — Dark branch)
```

A player pursues one branch, not both, unless doing intentional cross-school play.

### Pool tiers

T1 and T2 pools accept either energy type — Nox taints them (see above). T3 pools are **aligned**: they only accept their native energy type. Wrong energy input triggers a rejection — equal amounts of both the incoming energy and the pool's stored energy are lost.

```
Nox into Sacred Mana Pool  →  lose X Nox + X Mana (equal proportions)
Mana into Desecrated Pool  →  lose X Mana + X Nox (equal proportions)
```

| Pool | Accepts | Capacity | Unlocks |
|------|---------|----------|---------|
| Mana Pool | Mana or Nox (taints) | 1,000,000 | T1 infusion |
| Infused Mana Pool | Mana or Nox (taints) | 4,000,000 | T2 infusion, Infused materials |
| Sacred Mana Pool | **Mana only** | 16,000,000 | Sacred materials |
| Desecrated Pool | **Nox only** | 16,000,000 | Desecrated materials |

T3 pools are pure vessels — they resist contamination at a cost. This makes Sacred and Desecrated infrastructure genuinely distinct rather than just named differently.

## Shared Stations — Cross-School

The Petal Apothecary, Altar, and Weavery are **cross-school infrastructure**. None are Verdant-exclusive — any school can have recipes for them. The Verdant path builds them first; other schools add their own recipe support alongside their own content.

### Apothecary — fluid determines school

The Apothecary is filled with a fluid before use. The fluid type activates a different recipe set.

| Fluid | Colour | Recipe school | Notes |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Water | Blue | Verdant / floral | Default Verdant recipes |
| Lava | Orange | TBD school | TBD — fire/forge-adjacent path |
| Blood | Red | Sanguine | Sanguine school recipes |

A single Apothecary block can be used for all schools by changing what you fill it with. The tier (Petal / Infused / Sacred / Desecrated) gates recipe complexity; the fluid gates recipe school.

### Weavery — cross-school trinket merging

The Weavery (smithing-table style, tier-matched) accepts trinkets from any school. Recipe structure stays the same — template slot + cloth piece + trinket → augmented cloth. Other schools add their own trinket types with their own slot affinity and effects. Three tiers (Weavery / Infused / Sacred) serve all schools; a Desecrated tier is TBD.

### Altar — energy determines school

The Altar draws from a nearby pool. The energy type in that pool determines which recipes fire.

| Pool energy | Recipe school |
|-------------|---------------|
| Mana | Verdant / generic |
| Nox | Any non-Verdant school (cross-school dark energy) |

Physically the Desecrated Altar is the same station built from Desecrated Living Rock — it draws from a Desecrated Pool for Nox recipes. Same mechanic, dark medium.

## Station Tiers

All shared stations exist in four tiers. Tier gates recipe complexity (slot count / recipe label), not school.

### Apothecary

| Tier | Built from | School gate | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Petal Apothecary | Living Rock | All schools T1 | |
| Infused Apothecary | Infused Living Rock | All schools T2 | |
| Sacred Apothecary | Sacred Living Rock | Verdant T3 | |
| Desecrated Apothecary | Desecrated Living Rock | Dark T3 | Dark-flavour parallel |

### Altar

| Tier | Slots | Built from | Notes |
|------|-------|-----------|-------|
| Altar | 4 | Living Rock + gem | |
| Infused Altar | 6 | Infused Living Rock + Altar | |
| Sacred Altar | 16 | Sacred Living Rock + Infused Altar | |
| Desecrated Altar | 16 | Desecrated Living Rock + Infused Altar | Dark parallel |

Desecrated Altar: consumes Infused Altar in center slot (same upgrade recipe pattern), substituting Desecrated Living Rock as the surrounding material.
