# The Verdant Path

A gentle but foundational school of magic centered on **living mana, sacred gardens, floral rites, healing, and the quiet grace of the natural world**. Where the Rational Path builds engines and the Sanguine Path sanctifies blood and death, the Verdant Path cultivates life — not through domination, but through growth, patience, and attunement.

## Thematic Identity

The Verdant Path draws power from **living flowers, sacred groves, natural mana, and the lingering presence of unnamed divine-natural forces**. Its practitioners do not command nature so much as **coax it into bloom**. They cultivate sanctuaries, align themselves with gentle presences that dwell in root and petal, and create structures of living stone, mana-rich flora, and patient ritual.

This path should feel:

* **bright, soft, and restorative**
* **garden-like rather than industrial**
* **ritual and infrastructure oriented, but low on cruelty**
* **supportive rather than explosive**
* **beautiful, deliberate, and alive**

It is not “weak” — it is simply not interested in raw domination. Verdant power is the power to make a place flourish, to keep people alive, to purify corruption, and to build magic through living systems rather than sacrifice or force.

### Tone anchors

* flower fields threaded with mana-light
* living stone and carved garden shrines
* petal bowls, soft bells, and sacred pools
* drifting pollen, luminous dew, and quiet green glow
* mossy sanctums, sunlit groves, and fae-like natural grace
* unnamed divinities felt in bloom, root, and spring rather than worshipped directly

### Contrast with the other schools

* **Rational** assembles engines and suppresses instability; **Verdant** grows mana ecosystems and nurtures stable sacred spaces
* **Summoning** bargains with outsiders; **Verdant** aligns with the living world already present
* **Sanguine** draws on blood, death, and transformation; **Verdant** draws on growth, blessing, and gentle renewal

---

## Design Goals

### 1. Make this the approachable magic school

The Verdant Path should be the **cleanest and most welcoming entry point into magic** in the pack.

It should:

* introduce mana as magical infrastructure
* teach the player to build magical spaces rather than just cast abilities
* reward patience, placement, and cultivation
* feel safe and readable compared to darker schools

If a player wants “the nice magic,” this is the lane they should naturally gravitate toward.

### 2. Give it a full identity beyond “flowers = mana”

The Verdant Path should not just be “Botania progression with a new coat of paint.” It should feel like a complete school with its own:

* emotional tone
* resource loop
* environmental presence
* strengths and weaknesses
* relationship to the world and the other schools

### 3. Let it be powerful through sustain, sanctity, and space

Verdant power should come from:

* healing and protection
* passive mana generation
* garden infrastructure
* environmental cleansing and blessing
* creating places where life, safety, and magical stability are easier to maintain

### 4. Make it the “light magic” counterpart to Sanguine without becoming generic holy magic

The Verdant Path is **not** a church, not a paladin order, and not a named-god religion. It is sacred, but in a softer and more natural sense:

* unnamed divine-natural presence
* floral and fae-adjacent symbolism
* reverence without strict dogma
* beauty and vitality rather than moral judgment

---

## Core Fantasy

The Verdant Path is about **growing magical life into infrastructure**.

Instead of building machines or cutting deals, the player:

* gathers mystical flowers and natural reagents
* builds apothecaries, mana pools, and living stone structures
* cultivates gardens that generate and store mana
* uses that mana to heal, ward, cleanse, illuminate, and gently reshape the environment
* deepens their attunement to sacred natural spaces and the subtle presences within them

The school should support several player fantasies:

* **The Garden Mystic** — grows sacred floral infrastructure and lives inside a mana grove
* **The Warden of Green Places** — protects others with healing, wards, and sanctified terrain
* **The Floral Artisan** — crafts charms, petals, and living materials from cultivated flowers
* **The Grovekeeper** — builds sanctuaries that resist corruption and industrial harshness
* **The Fae-Touched Pilgrim** — deepens their alignment with divine-natural presences and becomes visibly “of the path”

---

## Verdant Exposure Counter

As a player invests in this school, they accumulate **Verdant Exposure**. This represents attunement to living mana, sacred floral presence, and the divine-natural rhythm of the Verdant Path.

Higher exposure is not corruption or punishment. It marks how deeply the player has been shaped by the path.

### Higher Verdant Exposure may:

* unlock deeper guidebook entries and advanced floral rites
* allow attunement to stronger Verdant relics and curios
* gate access to higher-tier flowers, blessings, or sanctified structures
* improve stability and efficiency in sacred groves and Verdant Aura zones
* mark the player as “of the path” for future inter-school interactions
* slightly alter how the player responds to corruption, blight, or certain ritual spaces

### Possible cosmetic changes later

* drifting pollen or mana motes around the player
* faint eye glow or flower-like particles while using mana
* flowers or moss subtly blooming in spaces the player spends time in
* a soft aura of light or fragrance in high Verdant states

### Possible side effects later

* discomfort in heavily industrial or death-saturated spaces
* reduced tolerance for blight, curse fields, or blood-heavy rituals
* certain dark rites becoming more unstable or morally incompatible if the player is deeply attuned

## Core Resource Model

The Verdant Path is built around **Mana**, **Flowers**, and **Sanctified Spaces**.

```text
Mystical Flowers / Natural Reagents / Sacred Seeds
  ↓ cultivated and processed through floral crafting
Generating Flowers / Living Structures / Mana Sources
  ↓
Mana Pools, Shrines, and Verdant Infrastructure
  ↓
Healing, Blessings, Utility, Wards, and Environmental Magic
```

### Primary Concepts

#### **Mana**

The fundamental energy of the school. Verdant mana is not abstract “spell points” — it is a living magical current cultivated through flowers, sacred structures, and careful tending.

Mana is used for:

* healing and protective effects
* floral crafting
* powering functional flowers or shrines
* activating blessings and utilities
* sustaining higher-tier Verdant structures

### **Mystical Flowers**

The visual and practical foundation of the path.

Flowers are:

* reagents
* decorative identity markers
* mana infrastructure
* ritual components
* a bridge between natural beauty and magical function

They should feel like more than collectibles — they are the building blocks of Verdant practice.

### **Living Materials**

Materials shaped by floral and sacred processes rather than by furnace or forge.

Examples:

* living stone / living rock
* living wood or verdant timber
* petal-crafted fibers, charms, and relic housings
* mana-infused metals or alloys later in progression

These materials help the Verdant Path feel architectural rather than just consumable.

### **Verdant Aura**

A local field generated by gardens, sacred pools, flowers, shrines, and other life-aligned structures.

Verdant Aura represents:

* a place becoming deeply attuned to life, growth, blessing, and natural mana
* a sanctuary where healing, cleansing, and floral magic are easier to sustain
* a gentle but persistent pressure against blight, corruption, and harsh industrialization

## The Three Pillars of the Path

The Verdant Path is one school, but it should internally feel like it has **three linked faces**.

# 1) Bloom

The language of flowers, petals, mana generation, and floral craft.

This includes:

* mystical flowers and petals
* mana generation and storage
* petal apothecary-style crafting
* living stone and floral materials
* the visual identity of flower magic itself

Bloom is the school’s **resource and infrastructure** pillar.

# 2) Grace

The language of healing, blessing, warding, comfort, and quiet support.

This includes:

* healing abilities and passive sustain
* gentle buffs and regeneration
* protective charms and warding circles
* cleansing of curses, blight, or corruption
* light, calm, and sanctuary-oriented utility

Grace is the school’s **support and protection** pillar.

# 3) Grove

The language of place, roots, sacred stone, shrines, and life-rich environments.

This includes:

* sacred groves and sanctified gardens
* aura-rich structures and environmental shaping
* resistance to corruption and industrial harshness
* shrine building, living architecture, and long-term magical space design
* the idea that a place can become holy simply through patient cultivation

Grove is the school’s **world-shaping and environmental** pillar.

### Weavery recipe shape

```
S S
W W   S = string   W = Livingwood (tier-matched)   → 1× Weavery (tier-matched)
W W
```

| Output          | W                  |
|-----------------|--------------------|
| Weavery         | Livingwood         |
| Infused Weavery | Infused Livingwood |
| Sacred Weavery  | Sacred Livingwood  |

Implementation: subclass `SmithingTableBlock`, override container title per tier, provide tier-matched model/texture. No GUI code needed — reuses `SmithingMenu` directly.

### Apothecary recipe shape

All three tiers share the same goblet pattern — 7 rock blocks + 1 petal. The Living Rock tier is the only gate; petal is the center ingredient throughout.

```
C P C
. C .   C = rock (tier-matched)   P = petal
C C C
```

| Output             | C                   |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Petal Apothecary   | Any rock            |
| Infused Apothecary | Infused Living Rock |
| Sacred Apothecary  | Sacred Living Rock  |

**Progression note:** T1 uses any rock because the player has no Living Rock yet — you need to craft a Pure Daisy in the Apothecary, place it, and let it convert stone → Living Rock before you can build a Mana Pool. The Apothecary must be craftable before that loop begins.

*Reference: `Botania/Xplat/src/generated/resources/data/botania/recipes/apothecary_livingrock.json`*

## Progression

## Early Progression

The Verdant Path should feel welcoming, tactile, and visibly rewarding from the beginning.

### Suggested flow

1. Discover mystical flowers in the world
2. Gather petals and simple natural reagents
3. craft a **Petal Apothecary**
4. create the first special flower or floral catalyst
5. use it to transform stone or wood into **living Verdant materials**
6. build the first **Mana Pool**
7. generate the first mana through floral means
8. craft a simple healing, lighting, or utility effect
9. gain the first level of **Verdant Exposure**

### Early gameplay goals

* teach the player that flowers are both beautiful and functional
* establish mana as a cultivated resource, not a combat bar
* let the player create a visibly magical garden early
* provide at least one satisfying supportive utility quickly

---

## Mid Progression

This is where Verdant becomes a proper school rather than just “flower crafting.”

### Midgame unlocks

* stable mana generation and storage
* functional flowers or equivalent structures that consume mana
* healing / warding / cleansing tools
* Verdant curios and support accessories
* shrine or grove mechanics that reward building dedicated sacred spaces
* better living materials and decorative identity

### Midgame gameplay goals

* establish a true garden workshop or mana sanctuary
* support multiplayer or base-building with healing and utility
* begin pushing back against blight, corruption, or interference from darker schools
* make the player feel like they are cultivating a real magical ecosystem

Examples:

* a flower or shrine that passively heals nearby allies
* a ward that reduces curse severity in a protected area
* a blessing that improves mana generation or stamina-like utility while inside a grove
* a living light source that brightens and beautifies sacred spaces

---

## Late Progression

Late-game Verdant should feel serene, abundant, and quietly powerful.

### Late-game unlocks

* advanced mana infrastructure and high-tier floral devices
* grove-wide blessings or aura projection
* stronger relics and curios
* environmental cleansing on a larger scale
* hybrid sacred-industrial or sacred-defensive structures if you choose to support crossover
* unique structures that make a base feel unmistakably Verdant

### Late-game fantasies

* building a great sanctuary that rivals an industrial district or dark sanctum
* becoming a true keeper of sacred green spaces
* sustaining allies and countering corruption at scale
* using beauty, patience, and life-rich infrastructure as a form of power

---

## Rite / Ability Categories

The Verdant Path should be organized around **blessings, floral craft, and sanctified utility**, not around high-damage spellcasting.

## 1) **Floral Craft**

These create flowers, living materials, and mana-linked components.

Examples:

* crafting special flowers
* transforming stone into living stone
* infusing materials with mana
* producing petal-based charms or offerings

## 2) **Blessings**

These grant healing, regeneration, calm, resistance, or other benevolent effects.

Examples:

* minor direct healing
* passive regeneration aura
* increased resistance to corruption or curse effects
* improved mana stability or growth while inside a Verdant space

## 3) **Wards**

These protect spaces, people, and structures.

Examples:

* a ward against blight or corruption
* a protective field around a shrine or grove
* reduction of hostile magical side effects in an area
* a charm that softens interference from another school

## 4) **Grove Rites**

These sanctify and shape the environment.

Examples:

* consecrate a grove as a Verdant site
* strengthen Verdant Aura in an area
* encourage flowers or moss to spread
* restore a damaged sacred space

## 5) **Living Utilities**

These are practical quality-of-life effects tied to floral mana.

Examples:

* place a temporary magical light
* grow a plant or accelerate a small natural process
* create a comfort effect near camp / base
* reveal hidden natural resources or life-aligned points of interest

## Verdant Aura

Verdant Aura is the school’s environmental signature.

Generated by:

* mystical flower gardens
* mana pools and floral infrastructure
* sacred shrines and altars
* consecrated groves and life-rich sanctums
* certain relics or high-attunement players later in progression

### What Verdant Aura represents

* a place becoming deeply aligned with life, calm, sacred growth, and living mana
* a space where blessings and healing come more easily
* a sanctuary that gently resists corruption, blight, and excessive industrial harshness

### Example effects

* improves healing and mana stability
* strengthens floral growth or flower-based effects
* reduces the severity of some curse or corruption effects
* may lower the efficiency of heavy industrial machinery if the area becomes too wild or spiritually protected
* can protect against some of the harsher consequences of Sanguine or Summoning magic

## Cross-School Interaction

## With the Rational Path

Verdant and Rational should feel like **growth vs control**, not pure enemies.

### Rational pressures on Verdant

* heavy industrial zones reduce passive mana generation and floral growth
* anti-magic devices can weaken blessings or make certain flowers less effective
* large machine districts make it harder to sustain a pure sacred grove without adaptation

### Verdant pressures on Rational

* sacred overgrowth or aura-rich groves may reduce machine efficiency or interfere with sterile industrial order
* some wards can protect against anti-magic pressure
* Verdant sanctuaries may require Rational players to build carefully if they want machines nearby

The feel should be:

> **living ecosystem versus engineered order**

not simply “magic loses to tech.”

---

## With the Summoning Path

These schools are not natural enemies, but they should feel very different in tone.

### Verdant pressures on Summoning

* calm sacred spaces may reduce summoning instability or discourage hostile entities
* some divine-natural spaces may reject infernal or exploitative contracts
* blessings may cleanse residue left by dangerous pacts

### Summoning pressures on Verdant

* infernal circles or active contracts may pollute otherwise serene spaces
* summoned entities may frighten or disrupt certain Verdant structures depending on balance

### Synergy ideas

* broker spirits may value flowers, petals, mana-rich materials, or living wood
* Verdant reagents could be used in safer summoning circles or stabilizing incense
* a hybrid player might use Verdant sanctums to soften the risks of darker ritual work

---

## With the Sanguine Path

This should be one of the strongest thematic contrasts in the pack.

### Verdant pressures on Sanguine

* blessings and sacred groves may calm corruption, weaken curse intensity, or resist blood-tainted aura
* Verdant sanctuaries may restore comfort and vitality lost to Sanguine excess
* life-rich spaces may reduce the efficiency of grave-heavy rites

### Sanguine pressures on Verdant

* blood sanctums and death-saturated areas may wither flowers, suppress blessings, or taint sacred spaces
* some curses may invert healing or growth if not cleansed
* deep Sanguine corruption should feel like an invasion of a living garden’s order

The feel should be:

> **life and bloom pressing back against blood, hunger, and death**

without making either side morally flat.

---

## Guidebook

A **Tome of Verdant Lore**, **Book of Bloom and Grove**, or similar guidebook serves as the school manual.

It should explain:

* the philosophy of the path
* how mana, flowers, and sanctified spaces work
* the three pillars: Bloom, Grace, and Grove
* how to craft early Verdant infrastructure
* how Verdant Exposure and Aura function
* how to create healing, blessing, and protective effects
* how Verdant magic interacts with the other schools

The tone should feel like:

* a gardener’s grimoire
* a field guide to sacred flora
* a gentle ritual manual passed between keepers of old groves

## Implementation Direction

This school does **not** need to replace Botania. It can begin as a **pack identity layer built on top of Botania-style progression**.

### Phase 1

* use Botania or a Botania-like mana foundation for early progression
* implement Verdant Exposure
* add a small set of Verdant blessings / wards / aura interactions
* make the school clearly “the light floral lane” in progression and presentation

### Phase 2

* add custom Verdant shrine / grove mechanics
* expand cross-school interactions
* create a few pack-specific relics, floral rites, or support structures
* deepen the sacred-grove identity beyond pure Botania reference

### Phase 3

* add stronger grove sanctification systems
* build hybrid interactions with science or resistance systems against dark schools
* create more custom school-specific world structures, Ponder scenes, and rituals

---

## Open Questions

* How explicitly **fae** should the path feel versus staying purely floral / divine-natural?
* How much of the school should be directly implemented through Botania systems versus pack-specific Verdant mechanics?
* Should Verdant have any real offensive identity at all, or stay almost entirely support / utility / environmental?
* How strongly should sacred groves push back against Rational machinery and Sanguine corruption?
* Should Verdant include tree / root / druidic motifs, or stay tightly focused on flowers, living stone, and mana?
* Are there special biomes, structures, or world events that should naturally favor Verdant progression?
