# The Sanguine Path

A dark ceremonial school of magic centered on **blood, soul remnants, funerary rites, vampiric transformation, and forbidden liturgy**. Where the Verdant Path nurtures life and the Summoning Path bargains with outsiders, the Sanguine Path turns inward and downward — into the body, the grave, memory, and the cost of changing what one is.

## Thematic Identity

The Sanguine Path is not casual spellcasting. It is **ritualized transgression**.

Its practitioners collect blood, ash, relics, and soul-tainted remnants. They carve circles, prepare altars, preserve remains, and perform rites beneath moonlight or in sanctified darkness. The school is not purely “evil,” but it is invasive, intimate, and costly. It asks what power can be drawn from mortality, memory, hunger, and transformation.

This path should feel like:

* **forbidden ceremony**, not fast magic
* **self-alteration**, not simple buff casting
* **grave-lit sanctums and relic halls**, not gardens or factories
* **power with residue**, where every rite leaves a mark

### Tone anchors

* blood sigils on pale stone
* soul lanterns and grave candles
* velvet-dark shrines and silver ritual tools
* reliquaries, ossuaries, black mirrors, moonlit basins
* vampiric nobility, funerary processions, sacred transgression
* spirit ash, bone dust, incense smoke, crimson thread

### Contrast with the other schools

* **Verdant** grows life; **Sanguine** harvests essence from life, death, and memory
* **Summoning** bargains with external beings; **Sanguine** transforms the self and manipulates what remains of the dead
* **Science** standardizes and stabilizes; **Sanguine** embraces contamination, mutation, and personal cost

---

## Design Goals

### 1. Dark magic without collapsing into “combat nuke school”

The Sanguine Path should not just be “the damage school.” Its identity is:

* ritual
* transformation
* curse
* vitality exchange
* soul manipulation
* long-term attunement to death, hunger, and forbidden knowledge

### 2. Separate it cleanly from Summoning

The Summoning Path says:

> “I make bargains with entities to get labor, trade, and service.”

The Sanguine Path says:

> “I perform rites that alter bodies, relics, places, and souls.”

Summoning is transactional and outward-facing.
Sanguine is ceremonial and inward-facing.

### 3. Make costs feel intimate

Costs should feel personal and material:

* blood
* vitality
* ritual scarring
* soul remnants
* corruption
* hunger
* moon-bound conditions
* altered relationships with other schools

### 4. Let the player become visibly “of the path”

This school should leave marks:

* pale skin, crimson eye glow, drifting ash, blood sigils, whispering relics
* altered food/healing interactions
* vampiric boons with social or environmental costs
* stronger affinity for night, ruins, graveyards, relics, and death-sites

## Core Fantasy

The Sanguine Path is about **using ceremonial power to transform the body, sanctify death, and weaponize forbidden knowledge**.

Instead of building machinery or growing mana flowers, the player:

* gathers blood, ash, bone, and soul remnants
* prepares altars and rite circles
* performs ceremonies to transmute materials, curse enemies, preserve souls, or alter their own nature
* manages corruption, ritual debt, and hunger for greater power

The school should support several player fantasies at once:

* **The Grave Ritualist** — builds sanctums and performs soul rites
* **The Crimson Ascetic** — sacrifices comfort and vitality for power
* **The Vampire Noble** — embraces hunger, blood, and nocturnal transformation
* **The Relic Keeper** — preserves memory, death, and spirit remnants in sacred objects
* **The Curse Scholar** — weaponizes misfortune, decay, and forbidden liturgy

---

## Sanguine Exposure Counter

As a player invests in this school, they accumulate **Sanguine Exposure**. This represents ritual scarring, blood attunement, spiritual contamination, and the gradual reshaping of the self by death-bound magic.

Higher exposure is not automatically “bad.” It is a measure of initiation into the path.

### Higher Sanguine Exposure may:

* unlock deeper guidebook entries and advanced rites
* allow attunement to stronger relics and curios
* gate vampiric or soulbound transformations
* increase compatibility with blood / grave / moonlit ritual structures
* alter how other schools react to the player
* make certain rites cheaper, stronger, or more stable

### Possible cosmetic changes later

* faint crimson sigils under the skin during rites
* pale or ashen complexion
* glowing eyes in darkness
* drifting ash motes or blood-colored particles
* ritual whispers / heartbeat audio in high exposure states

### Possible side effects later

* reduced comfort in Verdant sanctuaries
* increased sensitivity to sunlight or radiant effects if vampiric traits are embraced
* stronger reactions from anti-magic Rational infrastructure
* instability if the player abruptly abandons the path after deep attunement

## Core Resource Model

The Sanguine Path is built around **Blood**, **Remnants**, and **Rites**.

```text
Living Beings / Ritual Harvest / Grave Sites
  ↓
Blood / Ash / Bone / Soul Remnants
  ↓ prepared at altars, basins, reliquaries, and ritual circles
Sanguine Reagents / Relics / Rite Components
  ↓
Ceremonial Rites, Transformations, Curses, and Soulcraft
```

### Primary Concepts

#### **Blood**

A living reagent representing vitality, lineage, sacrifice, hunger, and oath.
Used in:

* ritual activation
* self-offering
* blood sigils
* transformation rites
* curse amplification
* vampiric contracts and relic feeding

Blood can come from:

* the player
* hostile mobs
* ritual harvesting
* special containers, vials, or preserved organs
* later: bloodline or species-specific sources

#### **Soul Remnants**

Echoes of death, memory, and spirit residue.
Used in:

* relic crafting
* funerary rites
* soul lanterns
* curse weaving
* transmutation of materials and identities

These should feel closer to **Malum-style spirit residue** than to “XP orbs but purple.”

#### **Ash / Bone / Grave Dust**

Stable ritual reagents tied to death, remains, and sacred ruin.
Used for:

* circle drawing
* incense and candles
* altar recipes
* preserving relics
* grounding rites in death rather than living mana

#### **Sanguine Aura**

A local field created by active rites, relic halls, blood altars, vampiric sanctums, and soul-forged structures.

This aura represents:

* blood resonance
* death-bound sanctity
* spiritual saturation
* transformation pressure on the surrounding space

## The Three Pillars of the Path

The Sanguine Path is one school, but internally it has **three strongly linked sub-themes**.

### 1) Blood

The language of vitality, sacrifice, lineage, hunger, and oath.

This includes:

* bloodletting rites
* self-sacrifice for power
* blood sigils and seals
* vampiric feeding or blood preservation
* vitality transfer and wound-binding

Blood is the most immediate and bodily part of the school.

### 2) Soul

The language of death, memory, funerary sanctity, and spirit remnants.

This includes:

* collecting soul remnants
* preserving echoes of the dead
* soul lanterns, reliquaries, and funerary bells
* rites of remembrance, extraction, and spirit shaping
* grave magic and relic empowerment

Soul is the school’s connection to **Malum-like spirit arcana**.

### 3) Transformation

The language of becoming other than human.

This includes:

* vampiric attunement
* ritual reshaping of body and senses
* temporary monstrous boons
* cursed gifts
* transmutation of flesh, appetite, and mortality
* reshaping items or materials through blood and soul fusion

Transformation is what makes the path feel dangerous and aspirational rather than merely morbid.

## Progression

## Early Progression

The player’s first steps into the Sanguine Path should feel transgressive but not overwhelming.

### Suggested flow

1. Discover a source of blood, grave ash, or soul residue
2. Craft a basic **Sanguine Basin**
3. Prepare a simple blood reagent or ritual ink
4. Create a **Rite Circle** using chalk / ash / candles / carved stone
5. Perform a minor rite:

    * reveal hidden death sites
    * preserve a soul remnant
    * curse a small area
    * create a weak relic or blood charm
6. Craft the first **Soul Lantern** or **Reliquary component**
7. Gain the first level of **Sanguine Exposure**

### Early gameplay goals

* teach the player that this path uses **prepared ritual spaces**
* introduce blood and soul as separate but linked resources
* give one small but memorable transformation or curse effect
* establish that rites have setup, timing, and atmosphere

---

## Mid Progression

This is where the school becomes a real identity rather than a side system.

### Midgame unlocks

* stable collection of blood and soul remnants
* first proper **Reliquary Altar**
* multi-step ceremonies with multiple reagents
* curse rites and blessing inversions
* stronger relic crafting
* basic vampiric or sanguine body modifications
* blood-infused materials or soul-forged components

### Midgame gameplay goals

* establish dedicated sanctums / crypts / rite halls
* choose whether to lean more into **blood**, **soul**, or **transformation**
* create rituals that change the player or the world, not just craft items
* introduce meaningful tension with other schools

Examples:

* a rite that converts damage taken into stored blood for a time
* a grave rite that captures a remnant from a slain creature
* a transformation that improves night vision but weakens daytime regeneration
* a curse placed on a location to hinder enemies or machines

---

## Late Progression

Late-game Sanguine magic should feel aristocratic, dangerous, and strange.

### Late-game unlocks

* advanced reliquaries and relic sets
* moon-phase or eclipse-sensitive rites
* major self-transformation / vampiric attunement
* powerful curse architecture
* large sanctums that project Sanguine Aura
* soul transmutation of rare materials
* hybrid blood-tech or blood-mana structures if you want cross-school synthesis later

### Late-game fantasies

* becoming a true nocturnal ritual lord / blood saint / death scholar
* maintaining a sanctum that rivals industrial districts or divine groves
* preserving and weaponizing the dead as memory, not as labor summons
* using blood and soul to rewrite what bodies, relics, and places can become

## Rite Categories

The Sanguine Path should be organized around **rites**, not around a giant generic spell list.

## 1) **Consecration Rites**

These prepare spaces, altars, relics, and tools.

Examples:

* consecrate a circle to blood, soul, moon, or shadow
* preserve a soul remnant in a lantern or reliquary
* mark a sanctum as a proper Sanguine site
* bind a mirror or basin to a player

## 2) **Curse Rites**

These impose decay, weakness, hunger, misfortune, or spiritual instability.

Examples:

* reduce healing in an area
* mark a target with a blood curse
* weaken summoned entities or machines depending on balance
* cause enemies to bleed essence on death

## 3) **Transformation Rites**

These alter body, appetite, perception, movement, or survivability.

Examples:

* gain stronger night vision and blood affinity
* become less nourished by normal food but empowered by blood
* temporarily become harder to kill while near a Sanguine sanctum
* alter movement, stealth, or regeneration at a cost

## 4) **Soulcraft Rites**

These manipulate remnants, relics, and memory.

Examples:

* preserve a soul remnant from a corpse or battle site
* transmute remnants into fuel for relics
* create funerary charms, spirit bells, or grave talismans
* extract echoes needed for advanced crafting

## 5) **Great Rites**

Long-form multiblock ceremonies with major consequences.

Examples:

* establish a vampiric covenant
* awaken a blood-bound relic
* sanctify a crypt as a major power site
* perform a transformation that permanently changes the player’s relationship to healing, hunger, or death

## Sanguine Aura

Sanguine Aura is the school’s environmental signature.

Generated by:

* active rites
* reliquary altars
* blood basins and soul lantern clusters
* sanctified crypts, shrines, and vampiric chambers
* certain transformed players or relics in high states of attunement

### What Sanguine Aura represents

* the saturation of a place with blood-memory, funerary sanctity, and transformative pressure
* a space where life and death are thinner and more negotiable than they should be

### Example effects

* empowers curses and transformation rites
* improves soul remnant retention
* weakens natural healing or ordinary food effectiveness
* suppresses delicate machinery or creates interference for precision devices
* may repel or distress certain Verdant effects if the area becomes too steeped in death

## Cross-School Interaction

## With the Verdant Path

The Verdant and Sanguine schools should be **opposed, but not simple enemies**.

### Verdant pressures on Sanguine

* sacred groves may calm corruption and weaken curse intensity
* life-heavy spaces may resist blood rites or reduce soul remnant yield
* Verdant sanctuaries can protect against the harsher side effects of Sanguine transformation

### Sanguine pressures on Verdant

* Sanguine Aura may suppress floral vitality and passive mana generation
* blood-tainted sanctums may wither sacred blooms or corrupt nature-aligned spaces
* some rites may invert healing, regeneration, or growth effects

This should feel like:

> **life and death pressing against each other**

not just “green school beats red school.”

---

## With the Summoning Path

These schools are cousins, not twins.

### Shared space

* both use circles, candles, and ceremonial setups
* both can live in dark sanctums and forbidden halls
* both can use rare offerings and atmosphere-sensitive ritual design

### Distinction

* Summoning uses circles to call and bind **others**
* Sanguine uses circles to alter **self, relic, corpse, or place**

### Synergy ideas

* summoned broker entities may trade in blood reagents, soul ash, or relic fragments
* certain circles or seals from Summoning may stabilize Sanguine great rites
* Sanguine rites may produce offerings or conditions valued by infernal patrons

### Tension ideas

* deep Sanguine sanctums may disturb or distort certain summoned beings
* infernal entities may dislike vampiric transformations that alter the practitioner’s “ownership” of their own blood or soul

---

## With the Rational Path

This should be one of the strongest conflicts in the pack.

### Rational pressures on Sanguine

* industrial anti-magic infrastructure can destabilize rites
* grounding pylons may suppress curse fields, soul retention, or blood resonance
* machine districts may make transformation rites more expensive or unreliable

### Sanguine pressures on Rational

* Sanguine Aura can foul precision machinery and reduce reliability
* curse architecture can disrupt industrial zones in subtle, persistent ways
* blood- or soul-based contamination may force science players to build purification / warding systems

### Hybrid possibility

Late game, you may allow dangerous hybrid constructs:

* blood-driven engines
* soul condensers
* anti-decay chambers
* reliquary housings inside machine frameworks

But these should feel like **taboo engineering**, not standard progression.

---

## Guidebook

A **Crimson Liturgy**, **Book of the Vein**, or **Sepulchral Manual** serves as the school’s guidebook.

It should explain:

* the school’s philosophy and taboos
* blood vs soul vs transformation systems
* how to build and consecrate rite circles
* how exposure works
* how to collect and use remnants responsibly
* what risks come with deeper attunement
* how Sanguine magic interacts with other schools

The guidebook should feel like a **ritual manual / inherited codex**, not a cheerful crafting pamphlet.

## Implementation Direction

This school does **not** need to be a giant bespoke magic system on day one. It can start as a layered implementation.

### Phase 1

* custom exposure value
* a small set of ritual structures
* blood and soul reagents
* 2–3 basic rites
* one curse, one relic, one transformation-lite mechanic
* basic aura interaction with Science and Verdant

### Phase 2

* reliquary progression
* stronger soulcraft
* first real vampiric branch
* larger sanctums and multiblock great rites
* better cross-school interactions

### Phase 3

* permanent transformation paths
* advanced curse architecture
* moon / night / eclipse-sensitive content
* hybrid tech-occult experiments

## Open Questions

* How explicit should the **vampiric** branch be — flavor, opt-in transformation, or a major late-game commitment?
* Is blood mostly a **resource**, or should there also be a strong **health-as-cost** mechanic?
* How much of the soul side should draw directly from Malum-like aesthetics versus your own funerary identity?
* Should Sanguine rites primarily affect the **player**, the **world**, or **items/relics**?
* How visible should corruption / attunement be to other players?
* Are there schools or items that should hard-counter vampiric or curse-heavy progression?
* Which parts are implemented by existing mods, and which are the pack’s custom identity layer?
