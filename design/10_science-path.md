# The Rational Path

A school of industry, measurement, and mechanical certainty centered on Create-style engineering, precision manufacturing, and the suppression of magical instability.

## Thematic Identity

The Rational Path does not view itself as "anti-magic" in a moral sense. Its practitioners believe the world should be **understood, measured, stabilized, and made reliable**. Where magical traditions trust intuition, divine attunement, bargains, or ritual, the Rational Path trusts systems that can be repeated and scaled.

* **Not mystical** — its power comes from structure, motion, and engineering
* **Plays like**: builder / machinist / industrialist / systems designer
* **Visual language**: brass, iron, belts, shafts, gauges, flywheels, drafting tables, glass instrumentation
* **Contrast with magic**: where magic grows, bargains, or invokes, science assembles, refines, and standardizes

## Rational Exposure Counter

As a player invests in this school, they accumulate **Rational Exposure**. This represents industrial discipline, static hardening, and adaptation to anti-magical environments.

Higher exposure:

* unlocks deeper guidebook entries and engineering schematics
* attunes the player to anti-magic curios and industrial safety gear
* reduces the penalties from operating in high Rational Aura zones
* marks the player as "of the machine" for future inter-school interactions

Possible cosmetic effects later:

* faint static particles
* metallic eye shimmer or brass-tinted goggles
* subtle mechanical audio cues near active machinery

## Core Fantasy

The Rational Path is about building systems that:

* process resources efficiently
* automate repetitive labor
* refine raw materials into standardized parts
* suppress magical unpredictability
* scale from workshop to factory

It should feel satisfying to solve problems with:

* gear ratios
* production lines
* mechanical logistics
* anti-magic containment
* engineered countermeasures

---

## Core Resource Model

The Rational Path is built around **kinetic power**, **processed materials**, and **stabilization infrastructure**.

```
Raw Materials
  ↓ crushing / washing / milling / pressing
Intermediate Components
  ↓ precision assembly / mechanical crafting / processing chains
Industrial Infrastructure
  ↓ powers anti-magic devices and high-tier production
Rational Devices / Hybrid Components
```

### Primary Concepts

* **Kinetic Power** — rotational power from Create machinery
* **Stress Capacity** — determines what industrial systems can support
* **Processed Materials** — brass, treated wood, steel-like alloys, precision parts
* **Rational Aura** — field generated by active industrial systems that suppresses magical instability

## Progression

### Early Progression

1. Gather basic materials and begin simple rotational setups
2. Build the first workshop and mechanical processing line
3. Produce standardized parts (plates, shafts, casings, precision components)
4. Craft an **Interference Meter** to observe magical vs scientific influence
5. Build a **Grounding Pylon** or equivalent anti-magic structure

### Mid Progression

1. Scale kinetic infrastructure
2. Automate processing of school-specific components
3. Build anti-magic industrial districts that can safely operate near magical zones
4. Unlock hybrid materials for working around magical interference
5. Begin using Rational structures to stabilize rituals or suppress hostile magical areas

### Late Progression

1. Build large industrial complexes with dedicated aura management
2. Produce advanced hybrid machinery that can safely function near magical schools
3. Manufacture tools that exploit magical weaknesses or convert magical outputs into industrial inputs
4. Become the dominant force in contested zones through infrastructure rather than raw combat

## Relationship to Create

The Rational Path is intended to **build on top of Create**, not replace it.

Create provides:

* rotational power
* mechanical logistics
* processing chains
* kinetic infrastructure

The Rational Path adds:

* school identity
* anti-magic engineering
* aura generation and suppression
* custom devices that interact with magical schools
* progression gates and hybrid tech

### Design Principle

Use Create for the **machinery fantasy**, and use the addon for the **science-vs-magic fantasy**.

---

## Rational Aura

Rational Aura is the school’s main environmental pressure.

Generated by:

* active high-stress machinery
* specialized Rational devices
* foundries, refineries, calibration structures
* anti-magic pylons and condensers

Effects on other schools may include:

* reducing passive mana generation
* increasing the cost of summons or rituals
* lowering floral growth rates for magical plants
* weakening curse intensity or ritual duration depending on balance

Effects on the Rational Path:

* improves machine reliability
* empowers anti-magic devices
* unlocks certain advanced industrial recipes or structures

---

## Cross-School Interaction

### Against Verdant

* Rational Aura suppresses passive mana generation and floral growth
* Verdant zones can reduce industrial efficiency if nature overwhelms infrastructure

### Against Summoning

* Grounding structures make summoning circles less stable
* summoned entities may weaken or cost more to maintain in high Rational zones

### Against Occult

* anti-magic devices reduce ritual stability and curse potency
* occult corruption may jam precision machinery or cause mechanical faults if not contained
