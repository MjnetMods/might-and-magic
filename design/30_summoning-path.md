# The Summoning Path

A transactional school of magic centered on contracts, offerings, circles, and summoned entities who trade, labor, carry, refine, and bargain.

## Thematic Identity

The Summoning Path does not draw power directly from flowers, divine grace, or personal sacrifice. Instead, it operates through **relationship, leverage, and negotiation** with beings from beyond the world.

Practitioners learn names, symbols, and offerings that attract entities willing to exchange service for payment. Some are minor household spirits; some are brokers and merchants; some are dangerous patrons who should not be called lightly.

* **Not a spell-slinger school** — it is about contracts and summoned utility
* **Plays like**: broker / binder / negotiator / occult logistician
* **Visual language**: chalk circles, candlelight, gold bowls, seals, incense, velvet-dark interiors, glowing sigils
* **Contrast with science**: where science builds machines to do work, this path hires the work from elsewhere

## Summoning Exposure Counter

As a player invests in this school, they accumulate **Summoning Exposure**. This represents infernal attention, contractual marks, and resonance with the beings of the Other Side.

Higher exposure:

* unlocks deeper sections of the summoning guidebook
* allows attunement to stronger contract curios and binding items
* influences which entities will answer a ritual
* marks the player as a known caller in future inter-school systems

Possible cosmetic effects later:

* faint sigils appearing around the player during rituals
* eyeshine in darkness
* whispers, drifting embers, or shadow motes in high exposure states

## Core Fantasy

The Summoning Path is about solving problems through **bargains**.

Instead of building every machine yourself or directly casting every effect, you:

* prepare a ritual space
* offer payment
* call an entity
* bind it to a task, location, or contract
* manage the consequences of that arrangement

The path should feel clever, flexible, and slightly dangerous.

---

## Core Resource Model

The school is built around **offerings**, **contracts**, **circles**, and **summoned labor**.

```
Offerings / Reagents / Symbols
  ↓ used in circles and contracts
Minor Summons
  ↓ perform utility tasks or unlock trade options
Bound Contracts / Better Seals
  ↓ stronger entities, more specialized labor, rare bargains
Advanced Summons / Patron Bargains
```

### Primary Concepts

* **Offerings** — items, food, metals, mana-infused goods, blood, curios, or rare materials used to attract entities
* **Seals / Contracts** — define what kind of entity can be called and what it is bound to do
* **Summons** — entities that trade, transport, gather, process, protect, or provide information
* **Summoning Aura** — a local field created by active circles, contracts, and bound entities

## Summon Categories

The school should have a recognizable roster of entity roles rather than a generic list of monsters.

### 1. Broker Spirits

Trade-focused entities who exchange items according to occult rules.

Examples:

* exchange common goods for rare reagents
* trade under moon phase or biome conditions
* offer dangerous bargains with side effects or hidden costs

### 2. Labor Spirits

Entities bound to simple repetitive work.

Examples:

* gather drops in an area
* move items between inventories
* refine one item into another over time
* harvest a small crop plot or collect nearby resources

### 3. Familiar Spirits

Personal helper entities with utility or passive support effects.

Examples:

* reveal nearby valuables or hidden blocks
* warn of hostile entities
* slightly improve ritual outcomes
* reduce the upkeep of nearby summons

### 4. Patron Entities

High-tier, dangerous beings who offer powerful bargains.

Examples:

* rare conversions
* major buffs or boons
* access to forbidden materials
* powerful labor contracts with steep upkeep or side effects

## Progression

### Early Progression

1. Gather chalk, candles, incense, and simple offerings
2. Create a basic summoning circle
3. Perform a minor ritual to call a weak helper spirit
4. Learn the first contract type (likely trade or item collection)
5. Build a **Contract Table** to formalize stronger bindings

### Mid Progression

1. Unlock multiple summon archetypes
2. Bind labor spirits to workshops, storerooms, or farms
3. Perform broker rituals for rare goods and magical reagents
4. Begin balancing upkeep, aura effects, and exposure
5. Learn to stabilize circles in hostile environments or near industrial districts

### Late Progression

1. Call patron entities and advanced labor spirits
2. Build dedicated summoning chambers and infernal markets
3. Negotiate contracts that affect entire production chains or ritual systems
4. Use summoned entities to bridge science and magic through specialized logistics
5. Manage the risks of debt, instability, and inter-school retaliation

## Trade Philosophy

This school should not feel like "villagers but spooky."

Trade should be:

* conditional
* flavorful
* occasionally risky
* tied to place, time, aura, or player exposure

Examples:

* a broker only appears at dusk
* an entity offers better trades in high Verdant aura but hates Rational zones
* one patron accepts mana-steel as an offering but rejects processed machine parts
* a trade may include a hidden drawback, debt marker, or temporary curse

## Summoning Aura

Summoning Aura is produced by:

* active circles
* bound spirits
* broker altars
* patron bargains
* contract infrastructure

Effects:

* may attract unusual entities or events
* can distort nearby machinery or make scientific devices less reliable
* can overlap positively or negatively with other magical schools depending on balance

The aura should feel less like corruption and more like **attention** — the world noticing that something beyond it has been invited in.

---

## Cross-School Interaction

### With Verdant

* certain broker spirits may value flowers, petals, mana-steel, or living materials
* Verdant zones may calm unstable summoning outcomes
* some divine-natural spaces may reject infernal patrons

### With Occult

* the Occult Path may provide stronger circles, harsher offerings, or blood-based amplification
* occult corruption may empower dangerous summons but increase risk

### With Science

* Rational Aura makes summoning less stable and may increase upkeep
* summoned labor can compete with industrial automation in small or medium-scale workflows
* hybrid late-game structures may allow stabilized summoning inside engineered facilities

## Guidebook

A **Book of Contracts** or **Ledger of the Other Side** serves as the school guide.

It should cover:

* ritual setup
* offerings and contract types
* summon categories
* broker rules and trade conditions
* danger signs, instability, and patron warnings
