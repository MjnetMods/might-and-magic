# Might and Magic: Science vs Magic

A modpack and companion addon centered on the tension between **industrial science** and **multiple magical traditions**. Players are not locked into one path, but every school leaves marks on the world and on the player. Machines suppress the wild and unpredictable; magic resists control, reshapes land, and distorts the reliable logic of industry.

## Core Thesis

The world contains two broad forces:

* **Science** — precision, engineering, standardization, repeatability, extraction, control
* **Magic** — living mana, ritual power, summoned entities, divine-natural influence, occult transformation

These are not simple opposites of "good" and "evil." Instead, they represent competing ways of shaping reality.

* Science seeks to **measure, contain, and reproduce**
* Magic seeks to **attune, bargain, awaken, and transform**

The player may specialize in one path, hybridize multiple schools, or deliberately force them into conflict.

---

## Major Schools

### The Rational Path

A science/industry school centered on Create-style machinery, mechanical production, stress networks, and anti-magic engineering.

* Builds factories, workshops, mills, refineries, foundries
* Excels at throughput, automation, item processing, and suppression of magical instability
* Weakens nearby magical effects through industrial presence

### The Verdant Path

A positive, floral school of magic centered on mana-bearing flowers, divine-natural alignment, and healing/supportive rituals.

* Grows gardens instead of factories
* Generates mana through living flora and sacred blooms
* Excels at sustain, utility, environmental shaping, and passive support

### The Summoning Path

A transactional school of magic centered on contracts, offerings, summoned servants, and trade with beings from beyond.

* Summons broker spirits, laborers, haulers, and dangerous patrons
* Excels at logistics, special trades, utility servants, and pact-driven progression
* Power comes with debt, attention, and obligations

### The Sanguine Path

A ritual-heavy school centered on circles, offerings, blood, curses, transformation, and forbidden workings.

* Performs deliberate rites rather than casual spell use
* Excels at powerful but costly effects, area control, corruption, debuffs, and long-form rituals
* Leaves lasting marks on the land and on the practitioner

## Design Goals

### 1. Multiple Distinct Magical Identities

Magic should not feel like one giant "spell tree." Each magical school must play differently:

* Verdant = growth, support, mana, flowers, grace
* Summoning = contracts, servants, trade, bargains
* Sanguine = rites, sacrifice, curses, transformation

### 2. Science Must Be a Real Path, Not Just Utility

Science is not merely "the mod that automates things." It should feel like a worldview:

* precise
* scalable
* suppressive
* cleanly understandable
* powerful through infrastructure rather than attunement

### 3. Conflict Should Be Mechanical, Not Just Lore

Science and magic should interfere with each other in tangible ways:

* machines can suppress magical growth or destabilize rituals
* strong magical zones can jam or weaken machines
* players can build stabilizers, insulators, and hybrid structures to manage this

### 4. Hybridization Should Be Possible but Earned

Players should be able to combine schools, but only through effort:

* anti-magic shielding
* ritual grounding
* special hybrid materials
* progression gates that require understanding both sides

## Exposure System

Each major school contributes a hidden or visible **Exposure** value on the player.

Exposure is not simply corruption or punishment. It represents how deeply the player has been shaped by a path.

### Examples

* **Verdant Exposure** — attunement to living mana and divine-natural presence
* **Summoning Exposure** — debt, contract marks, infernal attention, or otherworldly resonance
* **Sanguine Exposure** — ritual scarring, blood debt, corruption, forbidden insight
* **Rational Exposure** — industrial conditioning, static charge, rational discipline, anti-magic hardening

Exposure can be used for:

* guidebook unlocks
* Curios attunement
* cosmetic effects
* school-specific crafting or rituals
* cross-school interactions
* faction/reputation systems later

## Interference / Aura System

The pack’s central mechanical identity is **interference between schools**.

Each school produces a form of **aura** or **field** in the world. Aura is generated by structures, rituals, machines, flowers, and certain entities.

### Rational Aura

Produced by:

* active Create machinery
* stress-heavy industrial assemblies
* refineries, engines, foundries, precision devices
* anti-magic scientific infrastructure

Effects:

* weakens magical flora growth
* reduces ritual stability
* lowers passive mana generation
* makes summoned entities more expensive or unstable

### Verdant Aura

Produced by:

* mystical gardens
* special flowers
* mana pools and sacred groves
* living natural shrines

Effects:

* calms corruption
* improves healing and passive regeneration
* weakens industrial efficiency if the area becomes too wild or overgrown
* can protect against harsh Sanguine side effects

### Summoning Aura

Produced by:

* active contracts
* bound circles
* summoned entities
* broker altars and infernal trade sites

Effects:

* increases the chance of unusual events
* destabilizes nearby machinery or causes minor distortions
* may improve rare trade opportunities and pact outcomes

### Sanguine Aura

Produced by:

* ritual circles
* sacrificial altars
* curse totems
* transformation rites

Effects:

* suppresses natural growth
* taints areas for certain rituals
* disrupts precision machinery and delicate automation
* can empower other dark magical systems

## Progression Philosophy

The player should be able to begin with either side, but the paths feel different from the start.

### Example Early Openers

* Science: hand-cranked machines, simple rotational power, basic material processing
* Verdant: gather flowers, create first mana flower, craft petal apothecary, produce living stone
* Summoning: assemble a chalk circle, perform a minor calling, bind a tiny helper spirit
* Sanguine: craft ritual chalk or blood sigil, perform a small rite, create the first ritual focus

### Midgame

* Science scales into factories and anti-magic infrastructure
* Verdant scales into mana production and protective/supportive rituals
* Summoning scales into logistics spirits, trade entities, and contract specialization
* Sanguine scales into curses, large rites, area shaping, and dangerous transformations

### Lategame

* Hybrid structures and school crossover become possible
* Players can intentionally build science districts, sacred groves, sanguine sanctums, or infernal markets
* Specialized tools allow one school to resist or exploit another

## Content Ownership Philosophy

This project may use:

* **existing mods** for core systems (e.g. Create, Botania-like mechanics, summoning/ritual mods)
* **custom addon content** for the pack’s unique identity
* **data-driven tweaks** for balancing and integration

The custom addon should focus on:

* aura/interference systems
* exposure systems
* hybrid materials and stabilizers
* tutorials / Ponder scenes / guidebook integration
* pack-specific recipes and progression gates

---

## Intended Player Fantasy

The pack should support several fantasies:

* **The Gardener Mystic** — grows mana gardens, heals allies, protects sacred spaces
* **The Mechanist** — builds disciplined industrial infrastructure and suppresses chaos
* **The Binder** — bargains with entities for labor, secrets, and trade
* **The Ritualist** — reshapes the world through dangerous rites and sacrificial workings
* **The Hybrid** — attempts to force these incompatible systems into one empire
