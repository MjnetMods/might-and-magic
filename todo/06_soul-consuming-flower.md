# Soul-Consuming Flower (→ NOX)

A generating flower that consumes souls and generates NOX, mirroring [[04_blood-consuming-flower]] but keyed off the soul resource instead of Blood fluid.

**Why:** gives Summoning/Sanguine's soul mechanic an active NOX-generation use once souls exist as a resource, parallel to the Blood-consuming flower idea.

**Depends on:** [[05_summoning-souls-mechanic]] landing first — there's no soul resource for this flower to consume yet.
